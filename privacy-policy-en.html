Privacy Policy

Effective as of: 2026

Senanur Korkmaz (“Airlog”, “we”, “us” or “our”) provides Airlog, a mobile app for iOS and Android for tracking the TV shows, movies and books you watch or read, keeping lists, and sharing with other viewers (the “Service”). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we process through the Service, why, who sees it, and the choices you have.

Airlog at a glance

  • We do not show ads, we do not use advertising or analytics trackers, and we do not sell your personal information.
  • You need an account to use Airlog. Your watch history is kept on your device and synced to your account.
  • If you create an account, your library is synced to our cloud so it follows you across devices.
  • Some profile information and activity is public to other users (see “What other people can see”).
  • You can delete your account and all data associated with it at any time in Settings.

Regional notices for the U.S., the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, and Türkiye are at the end of this policy.

Personal information we collect

Information you provide to us

  • Account data: your email address and password or, if you choose Sign in with Apple, the identifier Apple assigns to you for Airlog and the email address (or Apple private relay address) you decide to share. We never see your password in readable form; it is stored by our authentication provider as a salted hash. You also choose a username when you first sign in. Your username is public; you can change it in Settings.
  • Profile data: the profile photo and cover photo you upload and your username.
  • Library and activity data: the shows, movies and books you add, their status (watching, watchlist, completed, dropped; for books: want to read, reading, read, did not finish), favorites, the episodes you mark as watched and when you marked them, your reading progress (current page, start and finish dates, the pages you log and when), your book ratings and private notes, your reading goal, your custom lists, the groups you join or create, the people you follow, and the badges you earn.
  • User content: comments (including whether you flagged them as containing spoilers), reactions, likes on comments, character ratings, and the name and description of groups you create.
  • Safety data: reports you submit about comments or users, and the users you block.
  • Pro waitlist: if you join it and allow notifications, your device’s push-notification token, plus any suggestion you write and your app language. We use it only to send you a notification when Airlog Pro launches; you can leave the list in the app at any time and the entry is then deleted.
  • Communications: messages you send us for support or privacy requests.
  • Photos you choose: if you pick a profile or cover photo, we receive only that image. We do not access the rest of your photo library.

Information collected automatically

  • Technical data: IP address, device type, operating system and version, app version, language setting and request times, which appear in server logs kept by our hosting providers.
  • Information on your device: your library cache, language choice and app settings are stored on your device. Notifications about new episodes are scheduled locally on your device. If you join the Pro waitlist and allow notifications, we also store your device’s push-notification token (see “Pro waitlist” above).

Information from third parties

  • TheTVDB: show and movie details, descriptions and posters. These requests go through our own server (a Supabase Edge Function), which forwards them to TheTVDB, so TheTVDB does not receive your IP address or account information; our server uses the request only to answer it. Metadata provided by TheTVDB.
  • Open Library: book search results, descriptions and covers are loaded directly from Open Library (openlibrary.org, a project of the Internet Archive). Open Library receives your IP address and the book titles, authors or ISBN numbers you search for or open, under its own privacy policy. We do not pass your account information to Open Library.

How we use your personal information

We use personal information to:

  • Provide the Service: create and maintain your account, store and sync your library, show your watch progress, statistics, calendar and lists.
  • Run social features: show your profile and comments to others, let people follow you, join groups, react and appear in leaderboards.
  • Personalize the app: show titles in your language, remember your settings and sort your lists by what you used most recently.
  • Keep Airlog safe: detect and prevent spam, abuse and fraud, review reports, enforce our rules and secure our systems.
  • Communicate with you: respond to support and privacy requests and send service messages (for example about your account).
  • Comply with the law and protect rights: meet legal obligations, respond to lawful requests and establish or defend legal claims.
  • Improve the Service: we may use aggregated and de-identified information, such as total episode counts, to maintain leaderboards and understand how features are used.

We do not use your personal information for advertising or marketing, we do not build advertising profiles, and we do not use it to train artificial-intelligence models.

What other people can see

Some information is public by design. Anyone using Airlog can see:

  • your username, profile photo and cover photo (image files are stored at public web addresses, so anyone who has the link can open them);
  • your comments, reactions and likes, and any character ratings you give;
  • who you follow and who follows you, and the badges you have earned;
  • groups you create or join, their members and shared watchlists;
  • your username and number of watched episodes if you appear in a leaderboard.

Your email address and your full watch history, tracked shows, movies and books, reading progress, ratings and notes, favorites and custom lists are not shown to other users. Content you make public can be copied, screen-captured or stored elsewhere by others, and we cannot control that.

How we share your personal information

We do not sell your personal information and we have no advertising partners. We share it only with:

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: Supabase (database, authentication and file storage hosting) and Expo (delivery of the single push notification we send when Pro launches, through Apple’s and Google’s notification services). They may only use the data to provide its services to us.
  • Open Library, which receives requests for book content directly from your device, as described above.
  • Other users and the public, for the information described in “What other people can see”.
  • Authorities and professional advisors where we believe in good faith it is required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety or property.
  • Business transferees if Airlog is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing or sale of assets; in that case we will require the recipient to honor this policy or notify you of changes.
  • Anyone you ask us to, with your instruction or consent.

Your choices and controls

  • Access and edit: view and update your username and photos in your profile and settings.
  • Delete content: delete your own comments, lists and other content in the app.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete Account permanently deletes your account and the data associated with it (profile, library, comments, reactions, lists, follows, groups you created and your Pro waitlist entry).
  • Get a copy of your data: email us at sena@senanurkorkmaz.com and we will send you your data in a common machine-readable format. You can also export your library from Settings.
  • Notifications: allow or block notifications in your device settings.
  • Block and report: block users or report comments and users inside the app.
  • Photo access: you can withdraw photo access in your device settings; this does not affect photos already uploaded.
  • Language: choose the app language in Settings → Language.
  • Declining to provide information: some information is needed to provide the Service. If you do not provide required information (such as an email address to create an account) we may not be able to provide the related features.

We do not currently respond to “Do Not Track” signals because the app does not track you across other services.

How long we keep your information

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the data associated with it is deleted from our systems; copies in backups are removed when the backups are overwritten in the ordinary backup cycle. Data stored on your device is removed when you sign out or uninstall the app. We may keep limited information longer where required by law, to resolve disputes, or to prevent abuse.

Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect your information, including encrypted connections, hashed passwords and database rules that restrict access to private data to its owner. However, no internet service is completely secure and we cannot guarantee the security of your personal information.

International data transfers

Our database is hosted in the European Union (Frankfurt, Germany). Our service providers and their sub-processors may process information in other countries, including the United States, where privacy laws may be different from those where you live. Where required, we rely on recognized transfer mechanisms such as adequacy decisions and standard contractual clauses (see the notice for European users below).

Children

Airlog is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us at sena@senanurkorkmaz.com and we will delete it.

Other sites and services

The Service may link to or show content from third parties, such as TheTVDB, Open Library or the app stores. We do not control them and are not responsible for their privacy practices; please read their policies.

Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make material changes we will update the effective date above and notify you in the app or by other appropriate means. Your continued use of the Service after a change means the updated policy applies.
